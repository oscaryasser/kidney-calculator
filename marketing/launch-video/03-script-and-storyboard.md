# Script and storyboard: "Watch your line" (30 s, 9:16 hero cut)

Narrator: warm female voice, mid-50s, unhurried, roughly 2 words per second. Music: a single soft piano or felt-piano motif, mixed low. Ambient sound from the scene stays audible.

Lead character: **Rosa**, 58, Latina, reading glasses pushed up in grey-streaked hair, cardigan over a plain tee. Same face, same outfit, in every shot. Second character: her daughter **Ana**, early 30s, only in shots 6 and 7.
