# My Kidney Team launch video

Everything needed to produce the 30-second launch video in Higgsfield with Seedance 2.5.

1. `01-research.md` — what the best health apps and videos of the last five years do, and the model facts that shaped the prompts.
2. `02-creative-brief.md` — audience, the one idea, three verbs, tone, medical guardrails, deliverables.
3. `03-script-and-storyboard.md` — shot-by-shot script with VO in English and Spanish, timing, and on-screen text.
4. `04-prompts-seedance-2.5.md` — the master 30-second prompt, per-shot repair prompts, character-still prompts, negative guidance.
5. `05-higgsfield-workflow.md` — step-by-step: references, characters, generation, screen replacement, edit, export.
6. `assets/` — the five app screenshots cropped to phone frames, ready to upload as references 1–5.

Concept in one line: **Kidneys don't hurt. They send numbers.** Three things anyone can do this week (check your pressure, pick the green ones, log your labs) and the line they add up to.
